Healthitude V1 — What to include
1. Patient registration & onboarding — YES

This is essential.

Registration

Legal first name
Middle name
Last name
Date of birth
Sex
Email
Phone number
Password
Address
State/LGA
Profile photo
Emergency contact

Health profile

Blood group — if known
Genotype — if known
Allergies
Existing conditions
Current medications
Previous major surgeries
Important medical history

But remember:

Patient-entered health information = Patient-reported

It does not become clinically verified until an authorized professional confirms it.

2. Patient category & verification — YES

This is already part of our model.

Patient
   ↓
Select ONE primary category
   ↓
Submit evidence
   ↓
Verification
   ↓
Pricing plan

Categories:

Student
NHIS
Civil Servant
Corporate
Insurance
Elderly
Nursing/Breastfeeding Mother
General

And:

Category → Pricing Plan → Service Price

3. Provider discovery — YES

This should be in V1.

Patients need to find qualified healthcare professionals.

Search/filter by:

Specialty
Doctor
Location
Availability
Consultation type
Price
Category/coverage where applicable

But the most important rule:

Only verified, active professionals with the appropriate scope appear as bookable providers.

4. Professional profile — YES

Every provider should have a public patient-facing profile containing:

Name
Photo
Profession
Specialty
Qualifications
Experience
Languages
Facility
Location
Consultation type
Consultation fee
Availability
Verification badge

This makes provider discovery actually useful.

5. Visit reason — YES

When booking, ask:

What do you need help with?

Examples:

General consultation
Fever
Headache
Stomach problem
Skin problem
Medication issue
Follow-up
Maternal care
Mental health
Other

This should not be a diagnosis.

It is simply the reason for the visit.

6. Appointment-specific intake — YES

This is one of the best things to take from Zocdoc.

After booking, the patient can provide:

Reason for visit
Symptoms/complaint
Duration
Severity
Relevant notes
Relevant documents
Referral document, if applicable

So:

BOOK APPOINTMENT
       ↓
APPOINTMENT INTAKE
       ↓
PROVIDER PREPARES
       ↓
CONSULTATION
7. Appointment management — YES

V1 should have:

Book
Confirm
Reschedule
Cancel
Appointment reminders
Upcoming appointments
Completed appointments
Cancelled appointments
No-show status

And continue using Calendly for V1 scheduling as previously decided.

8. Clinical records — YES

This is one of Healthitude's biggest differentiators.

Patient:

VIEW

Professional:

CREATE / UPDATE

Clinical records include:

Vitals
Clinical observations
Diagnosis
Treatment
Prescriptions
Lab results
Clinical notes

Patients cannot edit verified clinical records.

9. Patient-reported health information — YES

Keep this separate from clinical records.

For example:

Patient says:
"My blood pressure was 150/90 this morning."

             ↓

PATIENT-REPORTED

Doctor later measures:

BP: 148/92
Measured by Dr. X
14 Sept 2026

That becomes:

CLINICALLY VERIFIED

This distinction should absolutely be in V1.

10. Complaint → Primary Doctor → Specialist — YES

This is core Healthitude functionality.

Patient submits complaint
          ↓
Primary Doctor
          ↓
Can handle?
    ↙          ↘
  YES           NO
   ↓             ↓
Care        Specialist referral
                 ↓
       Qualified specialist
                 ↓
              Patient

This is much more aligned with Healthitude than simply being an appointment marketplace.

11. Professional verification — YES

Absolutely V1.

No verified professional should receive clinical privileges.

Verification should cover:

Identity
Qualification
Professional registration/licence
Specialty
Supporting documents
Expiry
Verification status

With:

PENDING
UNDER_REVIEW
VERIFIED
RESTRICTED
EXPIRED
SUSPENDED
REJECTED
REVOKED
12. Medication & adherence — YES

V1 should include:

Professional

Create prescription

Patient

View prescription
Medication schedule
Mark taken
Mark skipped
Medication reminders
Refill reminder

But no AI-driven drug interaction system in V1.

13. Emergency Break-Glass — YES

This is another major Healthitude differentiator.

PATIENT
   ↓
BREAK-GLASS
   ↓
Emergency request
   ↓
Verified qualified doctors alerted
   ↓
Doctor accepts
   ↓
Emergency Snapshot
   ↓
Emergency care
   ↓
Automatic audit

No previous provider relationship should block emergency assistance.

14. Emergency Snapshot — YES

The responding doctor should immediately see relevant critical information:

Patient identity
Age
Sex
Emergency contact
Blood group, if known
Allergies
Current medications
Major conditions
Recent relevant clinical records
Current complaint

Not necessarily the patient's entire history.

15. AI health assistant — YES, but limited

V1 AI can:

Explain medical terminology
Explain records
Give general health information
Explain medications
Help prepare questions for doctors
Navigate Healthitude
Help find appropriate care

But:

NO

Diagnosis
Prescription
Dose changes
Stopping medication
Replacing doctors
Emergency treatment

And AI needs the emergency/high-risk safety gateway.

16. Notifications — YES

V1:

Appointment reminder
Appointment confirmation
Complaint update
Referral notification
Prescription notification
Medication reminder
Professional verification notification
Emergency notification
Category verification notification
17. Audit logs — YES

This is non-negotiable because we're dealing with health data.

Track:

Who accessed a record
Who created a record
Who modified a record
Who viewed emergency information
Who verified a professional
Who changed permissions
Who initiated/accepted Break-Glass
When it happened
What I would NOT put in V1
